Culinary Delights

Jakarta Food I Can't Stop Thinking About

🍲

Soto Betawi

Rich, creamy broth filled with tender beef and served with warm rice and emping.

🥪

Roti Bakar Pak Eddy

Iconic late night toast loaded with cheese, chocolate, and nostalgic vibes.

🍳

Kerak Telor

Traditional Betawi spicy omelette made with glutinous rice and toasted coconut.

🍚

Nasi Goreng Samping Rumah

The street side fried rice right next to home that beats any restaurant version.

🍢

Sate Maranggi Depan Sekolah

Sweet & savory marinated beef skewers bought after school hours.

🍩

Kue Cucur

Sweet palm sugar fried traditional snack with crispy edges and a chewy center.

🥣

Lontong Sayur

Rice cakes served in savory chayote curry broth topped with crackers and tofu.

👵

Masakan Nenek

Comforting home cooked meals made with love by grandma that feel like home.

Shopping & Lifestyle Malls

Malls I Frequently Visit

Central Park Mall Featuring Tribeca Park outdoor green garden.
Grand Indonesia Massive shopping hub in the heart of Jakarta.

Waterfront & Culinary

PIK (Pantai Indah Kapuk)

Modern seaside area with aesthetic cafes, beach views, and food walks along the coast.

Vintage & Youth Culture

Blok M

The ultimate youth hangout district famous for Little Tokyo, thrift shops, vinyl records, and Japanese dining.

Historical District

Kota Tua Jakarta

Old Dutch colonial historic center featuring Fatahillah Square, street performers, and museums.
